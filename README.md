# Onion News Article Generation Model

This news article generator was trained on over 5,000 Onion News articles to let users create their own custom ones, in the exact same style of The Onion. No sense of humour required, a quirky headline is all that's needed.  

## Input 

Write a creative and wacky headline in the style of an Onion News article headline.

## Output

Receive the body text of the article.
